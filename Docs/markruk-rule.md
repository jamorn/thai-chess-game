Piece Movement Rules for Thai Chess (Makruk)

King (ขุน - Khun):

Movement: Moves 1 step in any direction (orthogonal or diagonal).

Note: Unlike Western Chess, there is no castling rule.

Rook (เรือ - Ruea):

Movement: Moves any number of unoccupied squares horizontally or vertically (along rows or columns).

Horse (ม้า - Ma):

Movement: Moves in an "L-shape" (2 squares in one orthogonal direction, then 1 square perpendicular to it).

Note: Can jump over other pieces, identical to the Knight in Western Chess.

Khon (โคน - Khon):

Movement: Moves 1 step diagonally in any direction (4 directions) or 1 step forward orthogonally. Total 5 possible moves.

Met (เม็ด - Met):

Movement: Moves 1 step diagonally in any direction (4 directions).

Pawn (เบี้ย - Bia):

Movement: Moves 1 step forward orthogonally. Captures 1 step diagonally forward.

Note: Does not have a 2-square initial double move.

Promoted Pawn (เบี้ยหงาย - Bia Ngai):

Promotion: When a Pawn reaches the opponent's 3rd rank (row 6 for Red/White, row 3 for Black), it promotes to a Promoted Pawn.

Movement: Gains the exact same movement capability as a Met (1 step diagonally in any direction).
